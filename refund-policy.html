Refund Policy

Last Updated:

Important: Donations made to JisnuSports are generally non-refundable as they are considered charitable contributions.

1. Donation Refund Policy

1.1 All donations made through JisnuSports are voluntary contributions towards our sports development programs and are typically non-refundable.

1.2 In exceptional circumstances, such as:

  • Unauthorized/fraudulent transactions
  • Technical errors resulting in incorrect donation amounts
  • Duplicate transactions

you may request a refund within 24hrs of the donation date.

2. Refund Process

2.1 Refund requests must be submitted in writing to jisnusports@gmail.com with:

  • Your full name
  • Donation date and amount
  • Transaction ID (from your receipt)
  • Reason for refund request

2.2 We will review your request within 10 business days and notify you of our decision.

2.3 Approved refunds will be processed to the original payment method within 14 business days.

3. Processing Fees

3.1 For approved refunds, any payment processing fees (typically 2%) will be deducted from the refund amount.
