Cookie Policy

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit websites. They help websites remember your preferences and improve your browsing experience.

2. How We Use Cookies

JisnuSports uses cookies for:

  • Essential Functionality: Login sessions and donation processing
  • Performance: Analyzing website traffic and usage patterns
  • Preferences: Remembering your language or region settings

3. Types of Cookies We Use

Cookie Type Purpose Duration
Session Cookies Maintain your donation session Until browser closes
Analytics Cookies Google Analytics tracking 1-2 years
Preference Cookies Remember your settings 1 year

4. Third-Party Cookies

We use these third-party services that may set cookies:

  • Google Analytics: Website usage statistics
  • RazorPay: Payment processing
  • Social Media: For sharing content

5. Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through:

  • Browser settings (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, etc.)
  • Our cookie consent banner (if implemented)
  • Third-party opt-out tools:
    • Google Analytics Opt-out

Note: Disabling cookies may affect website functionality, especially donation processing.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as our cookie usage changes. Please review periodically.
